The contribution here is the excitation-weighted common-kernel capacity penalty and its matched rank dependence at fixed finite local loss. The robustness and small operational examples apply established coding, continuity and task bounds under explicitly stated assumptions. A bounded literature comparison cannot establish that no further antecedent exists.
